# NOORVA — Brand System

**Premium ambient lighting for modern Moroccan homes.**

NOORVA = **Noor** (نور · light) + **Nova** (new star)

---

## Audience
- Age: 18–45
- Market: Morocco
- Languages: Arabic · French · English
- Currency: MAD
- Checkout: COD-first

## Products
Galaxy Projectors · Astronaut Projector · Sunset Lamp · Carousel Night Light · Home Decor · Room Decor · Gift Products · Gaming Lights · RGB Lights

---

## Color Palette

| Token | Hex | Role |
|-------|-----|------|
| Midnight | `#0B0E17` | Primary |
| Gold | `#C9A962` | Accent |
| Champagne | `#F5F0E8` | Surface |
| Soft Gray | `#A8A29E` | Muted |
| Pure White | `#FFFFFF` | Base |
| Nebula Violet | `#2A1B3D` | Mood / social |

## Typography
- **Display:** Cormorant Garamond (headlines, packaging, logo)
- **Body:** DM Sans (UI, email, WhatsApp)
- **Arabic:** Noto Sans Arabic

## Taglines
- FR: *La lumière qui transforme votre espace*
- AR: *نور يحوّل مساحتك*
- EN: *Light that transforms your space*

## Brand Voice
Calm · cinematic · confident · warm · never loud

**Say:** “Transform your room into a night sky.” / “Light that feels like a gift.”
**Never:** Cheap promo spam, emoji hype, “best price!!!”

---

## Packaging Layers
1. Matte midnight navy outer box + gold foil
2. Champagne tissue + gold seal
3. Soft-touch black product sleeve
4. Thank you card (Champagne)
5. Warranty card (Navy, 12 months)
6. Care insert + WhatsApp support

## Unboxing Ritual
Gold seal → tissue reveal → thank you card → product sleeve → warranty + care
