## Product Visual DNA (unique to this product)

A matte-black cylindrical base (Ø15 × 12cm, 450g) topped with a faceted, diamond-cut clear acrylic "crystal" dome lens — the defining silhouette of this product. A thin brushed-metal-look accent ring wraps the base rim, with a discreet row of circular Bluetooth speaker perforations on one side and minimal physical control buttons on the top rim. A slim black IR remote and a USB-A/Type-C cable accompany it. Signature light effect: a rich, multicolor RGB starfield and nebula-cloud wash across ceiling and walls, refracting into prismatic facet-lines as it passes through the crystal dome — more "boutique nightclub meets home planetarium" than cute-and-playful. Mood: moody, cinematic, tech-premium, music-reactive.
