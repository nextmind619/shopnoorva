## Product Visual DNA (unique to this product)

A glossy white, angular, multi-facet gemstone/diamond-shaped housing (16 × 9 × 10.5cm) — sharp geometric planes like a cut diamond rather than a rounded dome. A circular lens aperture sits at the front, ringed by a thin chrome or champagne-gold trim accent. A Type-C power port sits at the rear, and a slim black remote is included. Signature light effect: crisp pinpoint laser "stars" combined with sweeping aurora-borealis color washes (green-teal into violet into magenta), sharper and more graphic than the other two projectors' softer nebula clouds — the diamond-cut shell also casts crisp geometric shadow facets of its own silhouette. Mood: futuristic jewel, editorial, TikTok/Instagram-native, youthful premium — think "a gemstone that dreams in aurora light."
