## Product Visual DNA (unique to this product)

A cute chibi-proportioned astronaut figure, roughly 16–18cm tall, standing on small rounded white feet. A large round clear/faintly-tinted acrylic dome forms the "helmet" head, inside which the galaxy projector optics sit. Body is matte white ABS plastic with soft grey panel-line seams like a spacesuit. A black circular fabric-grille speaker sits on the chest. Small stubby articulated-looking arms rest at the sides. A compact black rectangular IR remote with colored silicone buttons accompanies it, plus a white USB cable. Signature light effect: a cinematic swirling blue-violet-teal galaxy/nebula with tiny pinpoint stars, projected outward from the helmet dome onto ceiling and walls. Overall personality: "cute but expensive" — a huggable sci-fi collectible, not a toy-aisle gadget.
