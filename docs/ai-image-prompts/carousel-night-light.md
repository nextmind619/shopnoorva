## Product Visual DNA (unique to this product)

An ornate carousel-shaped lamp: a soft pastel-pink canopy roof trimmed in gold filigree, supported by slender gold-tone posts, with tiny rotating bunny figures standing beneath a clear acrylic dome. The round base is gold-tone metal-look plastic with a soft velvet-textured ring and minimal physical buttons; a small 16-button remote and USB charging cable accompany it. Unlike the three galaxy projectors in this catalog, this product does **not** throw a projection onto ceilings/walls — its signature effect is a **soft, warm, ambient pink-and-gold glow** radiating gently from within the dome, paired with the **gentle rotation** of the carousel and its bunny figures, optional soft music, and a delicate rotating shadow-play on nearby surfaces. Mood: whimsical, tender, nostalgic fairground romance — a "heirloom music box," not a tech gadget. Several of the 30 image locations below are thoughtfully adapted from their literal titles to fit this product's kids/gift context tastefully (noted inline).
